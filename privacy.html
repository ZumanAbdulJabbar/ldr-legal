LDR: Love, Dare, Repeat

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

LDR: Love, Dare, Repeat ("LDR", "the app", "we") is a game app for couples. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. LDR is for adults aged 18 and over.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see them.
First name and character (boy or girl)Shown to your partner in games and on your profile.
18+ confirmationTo keep the app for adults only.
Pairing informationPairing codes, who you are paired with, when the pairing started or ended, and anyone you have blocked.
Game content you createYour answers (including typed text), drawings, words, and guesses, so the games work and your partner can see them.
Photos you chooseOnly if you play Photo Jigsaw and pick a photo from your gallery. The photo is uploaded to private storage that only you and your partner can access.
Scores and game historyCouple Score, streaks, round results and which questions you have already seen, so the app doesn't repeat them.
ReportsIf you report your partner, the reason and any details you write, so we can review it.
Drafts on your deviceUnsent answers are saved on your phone only, so you don't lose them if the app closes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, advertising ID or device identifiers. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases. The app only asks to access a photo when you choose one, using the system photo picker.

How information is shared

  • With your partner: your name, character, answers, drawings, words, photos and scores are visible to the person you are paired with. Nobody else using the app can see them.
  • Service provider: data is stored and processed by Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and file storage on our behalf.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of a user.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database access rules make sure only you and your current or former partner can read your shared couple data, and only the app owner can read reports.

How long we keep data

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Finished games are deleted automatically once both partners have seen the results; unfinished games are deleted after 24 hours of no activity. Scores and a list of questions already asked are kept so the app works well.
  • When you unpair, your shared history is archived. Either of you can delete an archived history in Settings → Past pairings.
  • Reports are kept for up to 12 months for safety review.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, pairings, games, answers, drawings, uploaded photos and score history. Because games are shared, deleting your account also deletes the shared history between you and your partners.

You can also request deletion without the app: see Delete your account.

Your rights

You can view and edit your name and character in the app, and delete your data at any time. To ask for a copy of your data or for any privacy request, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Children

LDR is only for people aged 18 or older and contains adult (18+) themes. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
