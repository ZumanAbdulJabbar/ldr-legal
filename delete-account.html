Love Dare Repeat: LDR Games

Delete your LDR account

This page explains how to delete your Love Dare Repeat: LDR Games account and the data linked to it.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open LDR and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Send an email to zuman0442@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my LDR account", from the email address you signed up with. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, password and sign-in data)
  • Your profile (name, character, 18+ confirmation)
  • Your pairings, pairing codes and blocks
  • All games, answers, drawings, words and photos from your pairings, and their score history. Because these are shared, they are removed for your partner too.

What may be kept

  • Reports you sent or that were made about you may be kept for up to 12 months for safety reasons, without a link to your deleted account.
  • Encrypted backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within about 7 days.

Want to remove only some data? You can delete a past pairing's shared history in Settings → Past pairings without deleting your account. See also our Privacy Policy.
